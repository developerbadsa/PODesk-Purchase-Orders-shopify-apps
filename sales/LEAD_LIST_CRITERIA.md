# Lead List Criteria

## Goal

Find merchants likely to care about Stocky migration, purchase orders, suppliers, and reorder workflows.

## Best Leads

High priority:

- Shopify POS stores
- multi-location retailers
- brands with retail stores and online store
- 300+ SKUs
- 2+ suppliers
- inventory-heavy categories
- public sold-out products
- stores mentioning Stocky in forums
- Shopify Community users asking about Stocky alternatives

## Best Niches

Start with:

- apparel
- footwear
- beauty
- supplements
- home goods
- gifts
- hobby products
- bike/outdoor/parts
- specialty retail

Why:

These niches often have variants, seasonal demand, physical stock, and supplier ordering.

## Bad Leads

Avoid:

- dropshipping-only stores
- print-on-demand stores
- digital products
- tiny 10-SKU stores
- free-app hunters
- enterprise brands already fully on NetSuite/SAP
- stores with no visible inventory depth

## Daily Target

For first 10 days:

- 20 new leads/day minimum
- 50 if possible
- 10 agency leads/day

## Lead Quality Score

Score each lead 1-5.

5:

- uses Stocky or asks about Stocky
- multiple locations
- 300+ SKUs
- public contact
- clear inventory pain

4:

- POS/retail
- likely many SKUs
- no direct Stocky proof

3:

- inventory-heavy Shopify store
- uncertain Stocky/POS signal

2:

- ecommerce store with some inventory
- weak fit

1:

- likely bad fit

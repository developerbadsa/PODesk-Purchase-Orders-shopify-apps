# Outreach Messages

## Rule

Do not spam.

Send useful, direct, short messages to merchants who likely have the problem.

## Message 1: Stocky User

Subject:

```text
Stocky shuts down Aug 31 - are your POs covered?
```

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

I saw that Shopify Stocky shuts down on August 31, 2026.

If your team uses Stocky for purchase orders, suppliers, stock counts, or reorder planning, the migration can be painful because supplier and PO workflows do not move cleanly.

I am building PODesk, a Shopify-native inventory and PO workflow tool. For early users I am doing a hands-on Stocky migration audit.

Can I send you a short Stocky migration checklist?
```

## Message 2: Shopify POS Store

Quick question about Shopify POS inventory

## Discovery Call Booking Message

```text
Thanks. I am doing 20-minute discovery calls with Stocky/POS merchants this week.

No sales pitch. I want to understand your current workflow and what breaks after Stocky.

If it looks like I can help, I can also offer a small migration audit.

Does [day/time] work?
```

## Paid Audit Offer

```text
Based on what you described, the riskiest parts are supplier mapping, PO history, and reorder assumptions.

I can do a Stocky migration audit for $299:

- review your current workflow
- inspect export files/screenshots
- map suppliers and PO process
- create a migration plan
- set up an early PODesk workspace if it fits

If we later convert you into the paid app, I will credit the $299 toward setup.
```

## What Not To Say

Avoid:

- "I built the best Stocky alternative"
- "AI-powered ERP"
- "We replace Cin7"
- "Fully automated inventory forecasting"
- "Guaranteed no stockouts"
- "Shopify approved Stocky replacement"

These claims create trust and policy risk.

## Best Tone

Use:

- direct
- specific
- useful
- humble
- operational

Do not use hype.

# User Discovery Plan

Goal:

> Prioritized MVP validation through user discovery interviews.

## Objective

Find out whether merchants have a painful, urgent, paid problem around Stocky migration, purchase orders, suppliers, and reorder workflows.

Do not ask if they "like the idea."

Ask about what they do today, where it breaks, and what they already pay for.

## Target Interview Count

First validation batch:

- 20 outreach replies
- 10 booked calls
- 5 completed discovery calls
- 3 real data/workflow samples
- 1 paid audit or paid pilot

## Who To Interview

Best:

- Shopify POS users
- merchants with retail + online stores
- stores with 300+ SKUs
- stores with multiple suppliers
- stores with multiple locations
- stores that used Stocky
- stores actively asking about Stocky alternatives

Avoid:

- dropshipping-only stores
- print-on-demand stores
- stores with fewer than 50 SKUs
- merchants asking only for free tools
- merchants already fully set up on NetSuite/SAP

## Interview Script

### Opening

Use this:

```text
I am not trying to sell you a full app today. I am researching how Stocky users are handling the shutdown and whether purchase orders, suppliers, and reorder workflows are breaking. I want to understand your current workflow.
```

### Questions

1. Are you using Stocky today or did you use it before?
2. What exactly did you use Stocky for?
3. Which part matters most: POs, suppliers, receiving, stock counts, reorder suggestions, transfers, or reports?
4. What will break when Stocky is gone?
5. Have you chosen a replacement?
6. Which tools did you compare?
7. Why did you reject any tools?
8. How many SKUs do you manage?
9. How many inventory locations do you have?
10. How many suppliers do you buy from?
11. How often do you create purchase orders?
12. Who creates POs in your company?
13. How do you decide what to reorder today?
14. What data do you trust for reorder decisions?
15. What data do you not trust?
16. What happens when you stock out?
17. What happens when you overbuy?
18. How do you track incoming inventory?
19. Do you need PO export, PDF, supplier email, or receiving?
20. Would you pay for help migrating your workflow before the deadline?

### Pricing Test

Ask directly:

```text
If someone could migrate your basic Stocky workflow, set up suppliers and POs, and give you a working reorder dashboard, would $299 setup plus $99/month be reasonable, too high, or too low?
```

Then ask:

```text
What price would make this an easy yes?
```

### Closing

Ask:

```text
Can you send a sample export or screenshot of your current workflow with private data removed?
```

Then:

```text
If I build a narrow version around this, would you be open to being one of the first pilot users?
```

## What To Listen For

Strong signals:

- "We use Stocky every week."
- "POs are critical."
- "We do not know what to use next."
- "Shopify native is not enough."
- "We need supplier data."
- "We need receiving."
- "We are using spreadsheets now and hate it."
- "Stockouts cost us money."
- "We would pay if migration is handled."

Weak signals:

- "Sounds cool."
- "Maybe later."
- "We only have a few SKUs."
- "We do not create POs."
- "We just need low stock alerts."
- "We only want free."

## Decision After 5 Calls

Build if pain clusters around:

- Stocky migration
- purchase orders
- supplier workflow
- receiving
- reorder decisions

Do not build if pain clusters only around:

- simple low stock alerts
- back in stock emails
- basic product hiding
- generic analytics

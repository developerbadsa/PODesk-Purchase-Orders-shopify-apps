# Product Principles

## Principle 1: Accuracy Before Features

Inventory apps lose trust when numbers are wrong.

Build fewer features with clear source data and assumptions.

## Principle 2: Read-First Before Write-Back

The MVP should not automatically change Shopify inventory.

Read data, calculate risk, create exports, and let the merchant decide.

## Principle 3: Explain The Math

Every reorder recommendation must show:

- sales period
- average daily sales
- available stock
- incoming stock
- supplier lead time
- safety stock
- suggested quantity

Do not hide behind "AI says so."

## Principle 4: CSV Is Not A Bad Thing

Early operations teams live in CSVs.

Support export/import well before building complex integrations.

## Principle 5: Manual Service Becomes Product

Early manual migration is not failure.

Every repeated manual step becomes a future feature.

## Principle 6: Do Not Become ERP Too Early

ERP scope will kill the first app.

Avoid:

- accounting ledger
- payroll
- full WMS
- full procurement suite
- production/MRP
- complex permissions before revenue

## Principle 7: Sell A Result

Do not sell "AI inventory platform."

Sell:

> Keep POs, suppliers, and reorder workflow running after Stocky.

## Principle 8: Measure Merchant Value

Track:

- POs created
- reorder recommendations viewed
- stockout risks found
- dead stock found
- hours saved
- setup completed

If the app cannot show value, churn will be high.

## 💡 Strategic Rationale

PODesk enters a crowded but price-sensitive category. Success depends on two things:

1. **Getting installed** — merchants must be able to evaluate the *entire* PO-to-receiving workflow before paying a dime.
2. **Getting upgraded** — when a store hits scale limits, the upgrade must feel like a relief, not a punishment.

Our strategy: **Give the core workflow away for free, monetize at the inflection points** — when SKU counts grow, when locations multiply, and when merchants want automation instead of manual oversight.

> 💬 *"Full Shopify purchase-order workflow free. Upgrade when your store grows."*

## 🚫 Do Not Build First

Features that look impressive but will *distract* from the core value proposition:

| Feature | Reason |
|:---|:------|
| AI demand forecasting | Too early before core reorder logic is trusted |
| Auto-reorder automation | Risky until merchants trust recommendations |
| Barcode scanning | Useful, but not launch-critical |
| API / webhooks | Better for later power users |
| Complex multi-location logic | High complexity and high support risk |

## 💰 Pricing vs. Willingness to Pay

> **Note:** APP_PLAN.md references a broader willingness to pay of **$29 – $199+/mo**. The $9.99 / $19.99 entry tier on Pro and Growth is intentionally set *low* to accelerate adoption and undercut competitors. As the product proves its ROI and adds power features, **price increases will be merit-based**, not arbitrary.

| Tier | Launch Price | Target ARPU Band | When to revisit |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Free | $0 | — | Never |
| Pro | $9.99 | $29–49 | After 50+ Pro customers |

## ✅ Final Recommendation

> Use a **generous Free plan** to maximize Shopify App Store installs and merchant trust. Charge for **scale, advanced operations, analytics, and automation** instead of locking the core purchasing workflow behind a paywall.

**Best launch positioning:**

> *Full Shopify purchase-order workflow free. Upgrade when your store grows.*

PODesk's pricing is not designed to capture maximum revenue on day one. It is designed to **capture maximum trust** on day one, and then **grow with the merchant** as their needs expand.

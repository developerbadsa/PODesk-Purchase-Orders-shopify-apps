## FAQ

### Is this a full Stocky clone?

No. PODesk focuses first on the highest-value workflows: suppliers, purchase orders, inventory visibility, and reorder planning.

### Will it automatically change my Shopify inventory?

Not in the first version. The first version is read-first and export-first to avoid risky automatic inventory changes.

### Do I need to be using Stocky?

No. Merchants using spreadsheets for POs and reorder planning can also use PODesk.

### Is this ready for enterprise?

No. Early access is for Shopify retailers who want a focused, guided workflow and are comfortable working with an early product.

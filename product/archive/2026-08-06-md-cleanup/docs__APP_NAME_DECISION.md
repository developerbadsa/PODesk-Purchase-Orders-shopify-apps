# App Name Decision

## Final Name

Internal project name:

> PODesk

Shopify App Store title:

> PODesk: Purchase Orders

Campaign:

> Stocky Rescue by PODesk

## Final Comparison: ReorderPilot vs PODesk

| Criteria | ReorderPilot | PODesk: Purchase Orders | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant understands instantly | Medium. Could mean customer reorder, B2B reorder, subscriptions, or internal restock. | High. Purchase Orders directly tells the backend workflow. | PODesk |
| Shopify App Store search intent | Medium-high. "Reorder" is useful, but broader and more ambiguous. | High. "Purchase Orders" is a direct merchant search phrase. | PODesk |
| Collision / confusion risk | Medium. PO Pilot and Restock Pilot are already close in the same category. | Lower. Exact PODesk app collision not found in Shopify App Store search. | PODesk |
| Brandability | Medium. Better sounding, but "Pilot" is overused. | Medium-low. Dry, but functional. | ReorderPilot |
| Long-term expansion | Better. Can expand beyond POs into reorder intelligence. | Good enough. Can still expand into inventory, reorder, suppliers, receiving. | ReorderPilot |
| First-app clarity | Medium. Needs subtitle to explain. | High. Name + keyword explains faster. | PODesk |

## Final Verdict

Use:

> **PODesk: Purchase Orders**

Reason:

The first app is not a broad commerce operating system yet. The first paid wedge is purchase orders, suppliers, Stocky migration, and reorder alerts. For that wedge, `PODesk: Purchase Orders` is easier for merchants to understand and more aligned with high-intent Shopify App Store searches.

Use `PODesk` for the internal folder/project name too. This keeps the codebase, docs, sales copy, and Shopify App Store positioning aligned.

## Why Not Stocky Rescue As App Name

Stocky is Shopify's product name. Using it as the primary app name can create trademark and App Store review risk.

It also makes the company look temporary. After Stocky shuts down, the name becomes dated.

Use "Stocky Rescue" only as a descriptive campaign, landing-page section, and SEO page.

## Why Not ReorderPilot Publicly

ReorderPilot is acceptable, but App Store search research shows "Pilot" is crowded in this exact category. Existing apps include PO Pilot and Restock Pilot, which are too close to our inventory + purchase-order direction.

ReorderPilot is rejected as the active project name.

## Why PODesk

PODesk is more back-office and purchase-order focused.

The app answers:

- what should I reorder?
- when should I reorder?
- how much should I reorder?
- which supplier should I reorder from?
- which purchase order should I create?

That is clearer than abstract names like Commerce OS, Ops Intelligence, or Inventory Brain, and less confusing than Pilot-based names.

## App Store Naming Pattern

Best format:

> Brand: Keyword Benefit

So:

> PODesk: Purchase Orders

This includes a distinctive brand plus clear merchant keywords.

# Shopify App Store Demo Video Shot List & Script

Product: **PODesk: Purchase Orders**  
Target Video Length: **2 to 3 minutes max** (120–180 seconds)  
Resolution: **1080p Full HD (1920x1080) at 60fps**  
Audio: Clear voiceover narration with subtle background music.  

---

## Video Timeline & Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:20)
- **Visual**: Screen recording opens on Shopify Admin dashboard. Voiceover introduces stockout frustrations and Stocky migration needs.
- **Action**: Mouse hovers over Shopify Admin sidebar and launches **PODesk: Purchase Orders**.
- **Voiceover**: *"Tired of managing replenishment with messy spreadsheets or worried about Stocky sunsetting? Meet PODesk: Purchase Orders—the read-only reorder and purchasing app embedded directly inside your Shopify admin."*

---

### Scene 2: Inventory & Catalog Sync (0:20 - 0:35)
- **Visual**: App Dashboard (`/app`). Focus on the **Sync Shopify inventory** button and summary metrics.
- **Action**: Click **Sync Shopify inventory**. Show real-time sync status progress bar completing with product, variant, and sales counts.
- **Voiceover**: *"With one click, PODesk syncs your products, active stocking locations, and recent sales velocity, calculating daily sales trends across your catalog without modifying your Shopify inventory."*

---

### Scene 3: Supplier Setup & SKU Mapping (0:35 - 0:55)
- **Visual**: Navigation to Suppliers (`/app/suppliers`) and SKU Mappings (`/app/mappings`).
- **Action**: Show supplier list with lead times and payment terms. Click into SKU Mappings, filter by supplier, and edit a mapping to set custom supplier SKU, unit cost, and 14-day lead time.
- **Voiceover**: *"Organize your supplier records in one place. Connect Shopify SKUs to suppliers with custom supplier SKUs, unit costs, lead times, and primary supplier rules."*

---

### Scene 4: Velocity-Based Reorder Table & Overrides (0:55 - 1:25)
- **Visual**: Reorder Planning screen (`/app/reorder`).
- **Action**: 
  1. Select 30-day sales window and set 7 buffer days.
  2. Point to critical stockout risk badges and clear merchant risk reason explanations.
  3. Enter a manual reorder quantity override in a table row and show the `"Manual override"` badge appear.
  4. Select checkboxes for two SKUs belonging to the same supplier.
  5. Click **Create Draft PO**.
- **Voiceover**: *"Our reorder planning table highlights stockout risks before they happen. Adjust demand windows, override calculated quantities for special promotions or MOQs, select at-risk SKUs, and create multi-line draft purchase orders instantly."*

---

### Scene 5: Multi-Line PO Details, Sharing & Printing (1:25 - 1:55)
- **Visual**: Purchase Order detail view (`/app/purchase-orders/:id`).
- **Action**: 
  1. Review draft PO line items, unit costs, subtotal, and expected arrival date.
  2. Click **Share / Copy Email** to show formatted email text and mailto launcher.
  3. Click **Print PO** to reveal the clean, merchant-branded printable PO view.
  4. Click **Mark as Sent** to update status to `SENT`.
- **Voiceover**: *"Manage multi-item purchase orders effortlessly. Generate clean printable POs for suppliers, copy pre-formatted email drafts, and track order status from draft to delivery."*

---

### Scene 6: Inventory Receiving & Progress Logs (1:55 - 2:20)
- **Visual**: Receiving action on Purchase Order detail page.
- **Action**: 
  1. Click **Record receipt**.
  2. Enter partial quantity received for line items.
  3. Click **Save Receipt**. Show status transition to `PARTIALLY RECEIVED`, progress bar update, and timestamped receipt log entry.
- **Voiceover**: *"When inventory arrives at your warehouse, record partial or full receipts line-by-line. PODesk tracks receiving progress and maintains complete receipt logs for total operational accuracy."*

---

### Scene 7: Stocky CSV Import Migration (2:20 - 2:45)
- **Visual**: Stocky Import page (`/app/imports`).
- **Action**: Show 3-step CSV import flow. Drag-and-drop a sample CSV file, show automatic column header matching, click preview validation, and execute import.
- **Voiceover**: *"Migrating from Stocky or spreadsheets? PODesk makes data migration seamless. Upload or paste your supplier CSV files with automatic column detection, row validation, and instant data import."*

---

### Scene 8: Call to Action & Outro (2:45 - 3:00)
- **Visual**: Return to Dashboard showing completed operations snapshot. Closing slide with PODesk wordmark text and Shopify App Store install prompt.
- **Action**: End on clean CTA screen with text: *"PODesk: Purchase Orders - Free Launch Beta"*.
- **Voiceover**: *"Take control of your inventory reordering and supplier purchasing today. Install PODesk: Purchase Orders 100% free during our launch beta on the Shopify App Store!"*

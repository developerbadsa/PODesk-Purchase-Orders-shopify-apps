## Long Description

### Stop Stockouts & Spreadsheet Chaos

**PODesk: Purchase Orders** gives Shopify merchants a simple, reliable purchase order and reorder planning workflow directly inside Shopify Admin. 

If you are currently managing supplier orders on spreadsheets, losing sales due to sudden stockouts, or looking for a clean replacement for Stocky, PODesk is built specifically for your store.

### Key Benefits

1. **Velocity-Based Reorder Planning**: Automatically calculates days of inventory left based on your 7, 14, 30, or 90-day sales velocity and supplier lead times.
2. **Manual Quantity Overrides**: Combine automated formula suggestions with your operational judgment. Set manual quantity overrides with custom notes without touching Shopify inventory.
3. **Multi-Row & Single-Row Draft POs**: Create single-line or multi-SKU draft purchase orders directly from your reorder planning table with one click.
4. **Professional Branded Purchase Orders**: Print or share beautifully formatted purchase orders with custom business headers, currency settings, payment terms, and supplier notes.
5. **Partial & Full Receiving**: Record received quantities against purchase order line items, monitor receiving progress bars, and log full receiving history.
6. **Stocky & CSV Import Tool**: Migration tool to import existing supplier lists and SKU mappings from Stocky or spreadsheets with file upload, live preview validation, sample template download, and invalid row exports.

## Feature Bullets

- Sync product variants, catalog SKUs, and sales history in one click.
- Supplier management database with lead times, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.
- Multi-supplier mapping per variant with custom supplier SKUs and unit cost overrides.
- Automated reorder risk alerts: Critical, Reorder Soon, Watch, and Healthy.
- Clear merchant-readable risk reasons for every SKU.
- Manual reorder quantity overrides with audit notes.
- Multi-row bulk draft PO creation for matching suppliers.

- Print-ready purchase orders with merchant branding and `@media print` layout.
- Line item partial receiving and full receiving log history.
- RFC-4180 compliant CSV importer with mapping auto-detection, preview validation, sample download, and invalid row export.
- Store-scoped settings for currency formatting, business address, and custom PO prefixes.

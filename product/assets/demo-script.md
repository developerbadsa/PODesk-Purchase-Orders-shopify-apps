# PODesk 3-Minute Product Demo Script

Date: 2026-08-02
Product: **PODesk: Purchase Orders**
Target Audience: Shopify merchants, retail operators, and Stocky users

---

## Video Overview

- **Target Duration**: 3 minutes (180 seconds)
- **Format**: Screen recording inside Shopify Admin with clean voiceover narration.
- **Key Message**: PODesk makes reorder planning and purchase order creation simple, accurate, and direct inside Shopify.

---

## Step-by-Step Script

### Section 1: Introduction & Dashboard (0:00 - 0:25)

**Visual**: Mouse lands on PODesk embedded dashboard inside Shopify Admin.
**Narration**:
> "Hi, if you run a growing Shopify store with multiple suppliers, managing stock replenishment on spreadsheets or disconnected tools can cost you thousands in lost sales and stockouts. Welcome to PODesk: Purchase Orders. PODesk lives directly inside your Shopify admin, giving you a clean operations snapshot of your inventory, open purchase orders, and urgent reorders."

**Action**: Click **Sync Inventory Now** button to demonstrate live catalog sync.

---

### Section 2: Supplier Setup & SKU Mapping (0:25 - 0:55)

**Visual**: Navigate to `/app/suppliers` and then `/app/mappings`.
**Narration**:
> "Setting up PODesk takes only minutes. First, add your suppliers with their default lead times, payment terms, and minimum order requirements. Next, map your Shopify variants to each supplier. You can assign custom supplier part numbers, negotiated unit costs, and specific lead time overrides. A single SKU can even have multiple suppliers, with one designated as primary."

**Action**: Demonstrate mapping a variant to a supplier and adjusting lead time.

---

### Section 3: Reorder Planning & Manual Overrides (0:55 - 1:35)

**Visual**: Navigate to `/app/reorder`.
**Narration**:
> "The heart of PODesk is Reorder Planning. PODesk analyzes your 30-day sales velocity and estimates stockout risk based on current inventory, target stock days, and supplier lead times. You can switch between 7, 14, 30, or 90-day velocity models.
>
> Every SKU gets a clear merchant-readable reason, such as 'Stock may run out before supplier lead time + buffer' or 'Already out of stock'. Need to adjust the suggested quantity for an upcoming promotion? Simply enter a manual override quantity and save a note. PODesk uses your final override quantity for purchase orders while leaving your Shopify inventory untouched."

**Action**: Change velocity window to 14 days, enter a manual override quantity, save it, and highlight the "Manual override" badge.

---

### Section 4: Multi-Row Purchase Order Creation & Print Output (1:35 - 2:15)

**Visual**: Check multiple rows for the same supplier on `/app/reorder` and click **Create draft PO from selected**.
**Narration**:
> "When you're ready to buy, select multiple SKUs belonging to the same supplier and click 'Create draft PO from selected'. PODesk automatically builds a multi-line draft purchase order, calculating the expected arrival date from the longest supplier lead time.
>
> From the PO detail page, you can review line costs, copy prefilled supplier emails, launch a mailto draft, or open the print view. The printable PO generates a professional business document formatted with your company details, address, payment terms, and line details."

**Action**: Create multi-row PO, click **Print PO**, and show the formatted print preview document.

---

### Section 5: Receiving Inventory & Stocky CSV Import (2:15 - 2:45)

**Visual**: Navigate to PO detail receiving section, then open `/app/imports`.
**Narration**:
> "When stock arrives at your warehouse or store, record received quantities directly on the purchase order. PODesk tracks partial receiving, updates progress bars, and logs full receiving history.
>
> If you are migrating from Stocky or spreadsheets, our CSV import tool makes it effortless. Download our sample CSV template, upload your file, preview row validation safely, and export any invalid rows for correction."

**Action**: Enter partial receiving quantity, click **Record receipt**, then switch to `/app/imports` and highlight the **Download sample CSV** button.

---

### Section 6: Free Beta Offer & Closing (2:45 - 3:00)

**Visual**: Return to Dashboard showing complete setup checklist.
**Narration**:
> "PODesk is 100% free during our open beta and works read-only with your Shopify inventory for complete safety. Install PODesk today from the Shopify App Store and take control of your purchasing workflow."

---

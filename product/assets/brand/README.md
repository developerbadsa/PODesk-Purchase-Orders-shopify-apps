# PODesk Brand Assets Guidelines

This folder contains the official production brand assets for **PODesk: Purchase Orders**.

---

## Brand Asset Catalog

- **`podesk-app-icon.png`**: Square (icon-only) brand asset. Used as the source for the Shopify App Store icon, favicon generation (`public/favicon.ico`, `public/favicon.png`), and small embedded in-app header branding (e.g. 28–36px in Shopify Admin).
- **`podesk-logo-horizontal.png`**: Horizontal brand logo combining the icon mark and the "PODesk" wordmark. Used in documentation, video ending slides, and public legal/support page headers (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/data-deletion`, `/support`).

---

## Usage Guidelines & Rules

1. **Shopify App Store Listing Icon**:
   - Always use `podesk-app-icon.png` (icon-only square) for the official Shopify App Store listing asset.
   - Do **not** use `podesk-logo-horizontal.png` as the App Store icon.

2. **Transparency**:
   - Transparent PNG is optional and not required by the Shopify App Store (solid dark backgrounds are standard and recommended for clarity).

3. **Pre-Submission Quality Check**:
   - Before final App Store submission, review the app icon image and remove any visible AI watermark or sparkle icon if still present on the asset corners.
